# DerpO channel bible

Channel: DerpO (@NotDerpO). Motorcycles and motorsport.

## Pillars
- **Think**: analysis and history. Why things happened (e.g. "What Killed the 600cc Supersport?", "WTF Happened To Yamaha").
- **Wrench**: hands-on mechanics, maintenance, mods.
- **Ride**: riding, trackdays, experiences.

Every video maps to exactly one pillar.

## Audience
Riders and motorsport fans, European perspective (Swiss-based creator), who know the basics and want the real story. Don't over-explain what a supersport is; do explain regulation and business context.

## Voice
<!-- COLIN: replace this section with 2-3 paragraphs from your own scripts. Examples beat descriptions. -->
- Direct, informed, a bit irreverent. Opinions are allowed when labelled as opinion and backed by facts.
- Strong cold open: a hook in the first 15 seconds, no "hey guys welcome back".
- Skits are welcome where they make a point (e.g. a meeting at manufacturer HQ).
- No hype words, no clickbait the video can't pay off.

## Visual rules
- On-screen avatar for Colin: his logo as the head on a real-life body. All people and bodies are real-life ("irl"), never cartoon bodies.
- Fonts: Barlow Black (900) for titles; a lighter but still bold Barlow weight for creator-credit handles.

## Accuracy
Every factual claim must be traceable to the research claims table. Opinion is fine; invented facts are not.
